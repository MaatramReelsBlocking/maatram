Maatram — bringing a change in you

Maatram is a free, open-source screen-time and focus platform built by students at SSVM School of Excellence. Instead of nagging you, it puts real friction between you and the apps that eat your day: a gate you have to wait at, a hard lock you cannot argue with, timers that reward the hours you stay off your phone, and study rooms where your friends can see the moment you slip. Inspired by Gen Z, made by Gen Z, made for Gen Z.
